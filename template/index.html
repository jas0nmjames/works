Hi, I'm Jason. Welcome.

Soon after I was promoted to User Experience Lead in April 2023, our UX Director included me in next-year budget conversations.

I discovered that we'd had money for training, but didn't have any scheduled because of the often (it was) arduous vendor management process at Santander.

I volunteered to lead the effort. Six months later, Nielsen Norman Group, Interaction Design Foundation and HFI Training were onboarded with 2023 funds.

After our Director was fired and shortly after a wave of layoffs, I worked with senior leadership to secure funding for our team for 2024. The result?

Outcomes

  • Two designers earned NN/g Master Certification
  • One researcher and one writer earned NN/g Certification
  • Three vendors onboarded
  • Funding secured for two years
