## Context (the bet)

Santander runs a group-wide strategy — *ONE Transformation*, "a digital bank with branches" — measured on customer growth, **NPS**, and cost-to-serve. Openbank is its 100%-digital arm. In late 2024 it entered the US: no branches, a market ruled by Chase/BofA/Wells, competing on experience and rate. The job was to make opening and trusting a brand-new digital bank effortless enough to win deposits at national scale.

- **Deposits:** $0 → $2B (Feb '25) → $6B (Oct '25) → **~$11.4B** (Jun '26).

- **NPS:** Santander US rose **6th → 3rd** (2024→2025), audited, vs. Chase/BofA/Wells/Capital One.
